## When you need a commercial license

- A business, or anyone working for one, runs SkillForge as its registry of agent artifacts

- A consultancy, agency or contractor uses SkillForge for client work that is not an open
  source project.
- You embed or redistribute SkillForge, or a modified version of it, in a commercial product,
  or offer it as part of a hosted or managed service.
- A business keeps using SkillForge after the 30-day evaluation.

You do not need one for noncommercial use, for open source projects as defined in
[SkillForge Additional Permissions](LICENSES/SkillForge-Additional-Permissions.md), or during
the evaluation period.

## Royalties

The royalty model and amounts are set in the commercial agreement. Typical plans:

| Plan | Scope | Royalty |
|---|---|---|
| Team | up to an agreed number of registry users | per user, per year |
| Organization | unlimited users in one organization and its affiliates | annual fee |
| OEM / redistribution | SkillForge embedded in, or distributed with, your product or service | share of revenue or per-unit royalty |

## How to obtain a license

Contact marin-ai technologies at https://github.com/marin-ai-tech (for a first contact without
confidential details you can also open an issue labelled `commercial-license` in

agreement is signed.

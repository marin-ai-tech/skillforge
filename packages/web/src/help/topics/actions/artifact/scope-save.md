## What it does

Saves visibility and owner team for the artifact after optional confirmation when narrowing or widening. Writes a scope_change audit event.

## When to use

Move an artifact into a team for privacy, or widen it back to org when it should be public to the registry.

## Requires

Admin; or owner who is a member of current and target teams; or lead of the current team. MCP tokens are rejected.

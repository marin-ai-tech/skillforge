## What it does

Two labelled lists above the feed: the kind (new artifacts, stable versions, status changes, posts) and the artifact type. Next to them, the checkbox **Only my artifacts** keeps only the news about artifacts you own, co-author or watch. Each choice is written into the address, for example `/news?kind=post` or `/news?mine=1`, and the feed reloads with it. Under any filter the pinned posts and the separator **Earlier — already seen** are not shown.

## When to use

Read only the posts of your admins, or only what happened to skills. When nothing matches, the page says so and offers **Reset filters**, which returns to the whole feed and works from the keyboard.

## Requires

Nothing beyond reading the feed; **Only my artifacts** is shown only when you are signed in. The filter never widens what you can see: rights are checked on every read.

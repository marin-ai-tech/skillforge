---
title: Filter the news feed
summary: Narrows the News feed to one kind of news or one artifact type. The filter lives in the page address, so it can be bookmarked and shared.
related: pages/news
---

## What it does

Two labelled lists above the feed: the kind (new artifacts, stable versions, status changes, posts) and the artifact type. Each choice is written into the address, for example `/news?kind=post`, and the feed reloads with it.

## When to use

Read only the posts of your admins, or only what happened to skills. When nothing matches, the page says so and offers **Reset filters**, which returns to the whole feed and works from the keyboard.

## Requires

Nothing beyond reading the feed. The filter never widens what you can see: rights are checked on every read.

## What it does

Opens the post form on the News page: a title, a Markdown text with a preview, the audience, the pin and important marks, an optional publication time and an optional end of display. The audience list only offers what you may choose.

## When to use

Announce a change to your team or to everyone: a new review rule, a planned upgrade, a recommended skill. Pin a post to keep it on top of the feed and on the Home tile.

## Requires

The `admin` role, or the lead role in at least one team. Other users do not see the button at all.

## What it does

Records the reviewer's decision for one merged finding. The four choices are Fix (correct the artifact), Verify (plausible but unproven — check it), Defer (postpone with a reason), and Reject (declined, duplicate, or a matter of taste). The decision is stored with the author on the review run.

## When to use

Use it after the run finishes and findings are merged by the judge, so each finding gets an explicit, auditable outcome instead of staying open.

## Requires

Role reviewer or admin. Viewers can read findings but cannot decide them.

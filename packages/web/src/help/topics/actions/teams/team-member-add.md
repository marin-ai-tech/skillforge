title: Add team member

related: getting-started/teams, actions/teams/team-member-remove
---

## What it does

Adds a user to the team (or updates their role). Default role is member; lead is also allowed. Idempotent when the user is already a member.

## When to use

Grant a collaborator access to the team's private artifacts, or promote a member to lead.

## Requires

Team lead or admin; target user must exist and be enabled. Team size limit is 500.

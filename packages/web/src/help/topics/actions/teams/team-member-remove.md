## What it does

Removes a user from the team. Their access to team-scoped artifacts ends on the next request. The last lead cannot be removed.

## When to use

Revoke access when someone leaves the group or was added by mistake.

## Requires

Team lead or admin. Removing the sole remaining lead returns last_lead and shows an error next to the action.
